## Important email integration note

The site is frontend-only. It cannot send an automatic email from an ImportNow sender address without a real verified sender address, an email provider (for example Resend, SendGrid or EmailJS), and the corresponding credentials/server-side integration. The enquiry form currently captures the visitor's email, opens the WhatsApp enquiry, and displays a thank-you confirmation. Do not publish a fabricated sender address or claim that an email was sent until that integration is configured.
