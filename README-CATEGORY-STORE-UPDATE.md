# ImportNow category catalogue update

- Removed the small pseudo-element decoration around form labels/inputs.
- Added clickable category cards for every item in “What we import”.
- Each category opens its own responsive product catalogue with product images, descriptions and product details.
- Added functional Add to cart/import-list and Wishlist controls. The cart count and saved-item count update as products are selected.
- Added a quote action that opens WhatsApp with the selected product names.
- Updated the Lighting, Supermarket & general goods, and Machinery & automation card video sources to Pexels stock clips: lighting retail display, fresh produce market, and industrial machinery footage. Video posters remain visible if a remote video cannot load.

Product examples are illustrative catalogue concepts, not confirmed supplier inventory or published prices. Confirm specifications, compliance and quotations with the supplier before ordering.
